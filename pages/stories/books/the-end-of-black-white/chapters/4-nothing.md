Three years have passed since the end of the monochrome era.

"Hey..." Someone called out to someone else in a dark alleyway, lit up only by neon signs at the middle of a night, "...Ferd."

He turned back to look at them, "Houston."

"Before we get back there to our showing, I would love to..." They gently spoke with a cigarette in their mouth.

"Get on with it." Ferd hissed.

"Just stop acting so stupid out there, 'kay? No audience surfing, no leaping at bands dressed in suggestive leather, no destroying instruments, no hate speech, no..." Houston counted their fingers as they went on.

"What's wrong with that?" He tilted his head.

Houston struggled to come up with a response, they've lived in a lawless land after all.

"Rich thems ain't got any fun, it's been years. Catch up." He riled them up, taking a sip from a glass bottle.

"I hate seeing you get hurt, bro." They looked at him with big eyes, "Each showing you're just sinking deeper man, and any time I try to reach out you just..."

Ferd walked up to them without a word, brushing over their cheek with a scarred right hand.

"...Proceed. Proceeding in unhealthy obsessions." They looked down as Ferd softly touched their cheek.

Ferd grabbed their head and kissed them without any prior thought. Houston from reflex raised their hands to struggle, but they didn't, they sunk deeper into the suffocating kiss stained by alcohol and ash.

"Feel better?" Ferd looked at them.

Houston blushed, though, they pushed him away. "Disgusting. Weirdo." They awkwardly chuckled, it clearly wasn't the first time.

"Maybe it's time you fuckin' let yourself go too." Ferd's eyes were cold, "No fun in staying lame, dumbass. You're wild, I saw it in you before, embrace it."

"...We have a showing to play in." Houston changed the topic swiftly.

"Yer right." He smiled before it quickly faded away, "Don't command me."

Moments later, with a mighty electric guitar riff, Houston and Ferd alongside two other band members were raised up on the stage to a wild roaring audience. Houston had a perfect angelic voice, of course they'd be the singer. Ferd with somewhat dexterous hands chose to be a guitarist, everyone assumes why but never actually ask. Ally stood at the back of the audience with a jaded expression and large eyebags, giving Sammy a piggyback ride so she can see her father... "shine". What once was a giant factory which destroyed the land they stand on is now a concert stage for wastelanders to relish in. The instruments kicked in, the drugged audience cheered, and Houston began to sing the wildly inappropriate lyrics to a song Ferd personally wrote, as always. Ferd played on the guitar like as if his life depended on it, his vision blurred with each stroke, his ears rang from the noise. Houston took the microphone into their hands as they evaded a glass bottle thrown likely unintentionally at them. Sammy innocently listened to the booming music, waving a red glowstick in her hand to cheer the band on. Ferd continued to play, chugging at a beer bottle halfway through, his vision proceeded to blur more, his ears rang more, until there was nothing left to see. His soul played the guitar, his body nothing but a vessel. He drooled, but kept smiling, he relished this moment to its end as his mind wobbled in the waves of nothing.

"...Houston, you fruitcake you did a great job." Ferd groaned, giving Houston a long rough pat on the head, "You two get outta here!"

The bassist and drummer packed their bags and left without a word to say, from their expressions alone it was clear they hated working with him.

"What are you gonna do if they leave, too?" Houston looked at them and then back at Ferd with a tired look.

"We find someone else." Ferd lit a cigarette, "Maybe Ally can stop being such a pussy and become our drummer, she got the anger for that."

Houston recoiled at his remark on Ally, but brushed it off quickly after, "I don't know about that, chief." They shook their head.

"Whatever." Ferd tossed his keys to their hands, "Meet me at my room in the afternoon, let's have fun."

Houston blushed and gulped, "...Uh, hell yeah, dude."

Ferd wandered off, grinning, mumbling something under his breath. Houston looked at him with simple frustrated eyes. "It can't go on, I will..." They whispered to themself until a dog licked and sniffed their foot.

"What's up buddy?" They smiled, brushing the golden retriever's ears, "Found anythin' new?"

The dog which seemed to have a missing eye, a blade for a leg, and scars all over its body pointed at a general direction with its nose.

Right after any concert showings, Ferd would always visit the local graveyard. Walking on and over any other tombstones and wooden crosses, he always made sure pay as much respect to the one which mattered to him most.

"Hi Ferd." Ally politely greeted him as he barged into their home.

"Hey." Somehow, Ferd also greeted her normally.

He picked up Sammy into his hands and gave her a forehead kiss. Ally was busy cooking up food, ignoring that their home looked like a complete wreck inside, as usual. Sammy has her fun observing flies with her big eyes.

"How drunk did ya get this time, dumbass." Ally looked back to the food, there it was.

"How drunk I got?" He looked at her with a superior look, "Like, super! Nothings wrong with that!"

"Surprised you even managed to stumble into our home. Feels like every day you just keep forgetting about us." She stirred the cooking pot.

"Nah nah nah." Ferd shook his head, audibly disagreeing, "You have a giant fuckin' walker dear, a giant fuckin' walker! Impossible to miss, besides I keep thinking about you all the time! When are you joining our band?!"

"When Houston dies." She growled, "I want nothing with that guy."

"Still on that, eh?" He laughed, "Get over it!"

Ally wanted to say something, but didn't. "Someday." She groaned.

"Right now maybe." He mumbled to himself.

Sammy was placing bets on the table, to what she was placing her imaginary bets for no one knew.

"How many funds did we get from Johnny." He sat down next to Sammy.

"Hmm..." She thought back to earlier today, "The town felt generous today, we got enough to finally fix the lamps."

"Great."

"Oh, the roads, maybe we should focus on the roads." She thought even more, forgetting about her cooking pot. Sammy swiftly took a stool and continued stirring for her.

Suddenly, keys were heard jingling behind their main door. The door opened, the whole family looked.

Houston awkwardly stood there with their long impish tail, "...Hi."

"GET OUTTA HERE!!!" Ally snapped and flung a glass bottle directly at their face. They shut the door instantly.

Silence ensued.

"Whatever." Ally rubbed her eyes as she shook her head in disappointment. Food was ready to be served. They ate it, shared some things about their days, Ferd almost fell asleep multiple times throughout the ongoing conversation, with Ally waking him up sometimes with a slap to the back. The lamp above the table blinked, who knows how long the lightbulb has been working there for.

"...Meet me at the lake at night." She vaguely told him.

He was confused tired, but willing to see what was up. He finished the last remaining scrapes of his bowl and went outside to check what Houston was doing.

Time went on, their fractured relations stayed. Ally spent most of her time on top of her giant walker gazing at the stars and clouds, but only when the full moon shined nicely. The winds were strong, each day that passed on the wasteland began to look more like habitable land. Beautiful, thin trees covered most of the surface. There was hope, she clung to it, gazing at the stars with Sammy by her side. She blushed from a thought, but disregarded it as she began to head for the lake.

Ferd chose to go the lake at the same time, too. Surrounded by palm trees, he saw her, the silhouette of Ally, covered in a towel which waved with the wind. In her signature of freedom, she took the towel off, naked, gracefully walking into the glowing water. Ferd stood there, looking at her blankly, she swam for a moment before turning back to his hyperventilating face.

"You wanted to have fun, I know it." She grinned.

As her feet kicked in the water, she laid out two soft hands for him to take.

"Come swim with me, idiot."
