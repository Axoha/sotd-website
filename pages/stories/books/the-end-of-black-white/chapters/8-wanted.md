Like a shooting star, he blazed through the darkness, leaving a trail of sparkling white dust wherever he flew. His two grand pair of red wings grew another smaller pair, he could masterfully control them at will, avoiding large black spikes emerged from the floor seemingly a long time ago. Large eyes have awakened in the darkness, and with it, whole incomprehensible beasts were summoned with it. The great waves of the deep sea below crashed onto the monoliths anytime, anywhere, in a chaotic unstable manner. With the beasts' giant eyes being open, producing enough light to see far away, waving strings across the whole sky reflected its light, and soon, shined white with stars surrounding each and every line. The hands were blazingly, relentless, in a matter of seconds he was already shrouded in darkness.

He just smiled, "No thanks!" Before producing enough light from his chest to break free from its grasp. "Sorry, there is something really urgent I need to get back to!"

Well, nothing could understand what he was saying, he knew that.

He swiftly turned back to his mission, flying straight up into the cloudy sky. With a swift move he made a gauntlet, and started punching away at the hands which followed him tied in faded strings. Like a child pretending to be a hero, with each punch he yelled a nonsensical word to keep himself pumped. "Ferd-tastic!"

But at moments when he felt a flicker coming on, he let his gauntlet perish and gave it his all, charging and producing enough light to absolutely repel everything of darkness close to him. This would really tire him out, but as the flicker came on and Ferd came back, in that moment where he'd just hopelessly fall through the void he could easily recover as he waited for his turn.

"Ferd. Ferd. Ferd." He flickered back to red, shaking his head, extruding four great wings to soar back into the sky, "I'm not letting you give up, forget that."

Leviathans, swam around the strings, many of the great giant Inka were busy examining the strings and keeping check of any problems. Each one that he would fly close to would temporarily quit their job to focus on the bigger problem.

"Any of you nice ladies wanna tell me where the place I'm from is?" He spoke and ended it with an annoying smirk.

He sensed a small Inka pointing at a torn string somewhere far.

"Nope, not that one, sorry!" He faced it as he flew, "Actually..." He gave it some thought as he evaded various strikes and beams from whatever was after him, "Thanks!"

The string right next to the torn one was the largest, should've been obvious from the start that it was the one. He instantly flew towards it. Given how far it was, he grew four more wings to accelerate even further through the void, unsure if Ferd would survive this speed. Well, not like he was mortal anymore, he could probably survive anything.

He flickered back to black, before was a typical explosion of light, and he fell through the void as Ferd was absolutely willing to give up. The burden on his heart too heavy. This time, however, Hazy appeared from behind Ferd and swung her blade with all her might to hit him. He wasn't red like she was planning, so she just sent him flying far from the big string as she held her blade like a baseball bat, confused.

Once he flickered back to red, lodged in another waving string, Hazy appeared before him. "I didn't appreciate that, y'know?" He coughed, moving slowly to get unstuck from the string's small crater.

"What the hell do you think you are doing?!" Hazy broke her character, baffled at his actions so far.

"I don't know, trying to live maybe!" He grinned, "Nice of you to start a small chat, I was really getting tired of all this fighting."

"But that's not how...!" She fumed, gesturing to express her anger, "You idiot!"

"Sorry, someone very dear to me is waiting. I need to go back." His smile left a sparkle behind, "And I'll be taking this, thanks."

With eight wings he blasted off again, taking Hazy's blade from her hands upon launch. The string was so far, he multiplied his wings to sixteen to go even faster. He couldn't waste time, not with the blade in hand, not with Hazy after him. It was not fast enough, he multiplied his wings again to go absurdly fast to a point even he could barely handle.

He crashed into his string like a wild bird to a window. It hurt really badly, but he only scoffed it away and retrieved the blade to try. With a determined swing, full of desire and anguish, he hit the string with all the force. Nothing happened, he tried again, but the blade only shattered in response.

"No, like...!" He thought, "T-This string's got nothing on me!" He desperately smiled.

He threw the hilt of the blade away and created two golden gauntlets for both of his arms. In the next second he was clawing at the string, as if he were finding the edge of tape. Clawing obviously didn't work, so he regressed to desperate powerful punches. Without him noticing, Hazy was sat on the string, eating noodles and watching his struggle.

"Are you done yet?" She asked.

He was getting more tired, "Never! Who do you think I am?!"

"A little guy who can never accept death." She answered honestly.

"Okay..." He looked back to where he was punching, trying even more.

"What did this world do to you?" She asked again, after she was done slurping her noodles, "This is our most important one, you are making all my sisters very upset."

He didn't answer, just struggled more.

"You might be forgetting there is a force much more powerful than both of us." She put the bowl to her side, "This is one of those places. You are an Angel, that does not mean every place can be bent to your rules."

"There's no time for a chat!" He slammed a grand hammer into the string.

"I was wondering about the flowers growing from your chest." She pointed at him, "Not even I can tell what that is supposed to be, care to tell me?"

Something sparked within him. Maybe his other-self could enter, not him. All he would need to do was wait, while standing on the string.

"Actually, I don't know either." He sighed, choosing to sit across Hazy, "Why are you talking to me?"

"I am waiting for you to turn back to Ferd."

"Oh." He looked away, "Okay."

It was slightly tense, Hazy's four eyes which never blinked felt like a stab every time he chose to look at her face.

"Are the flowers for aesthetics?" She pointed at his chest, "It does not match you."

"They just appeared." He gently touched the black and white flowers, "How come you of all beings don't know what this is?"

"We like flowers. I have a hunch on what this could be, but..."

They both sensed his imminent change to black. As if the talk never happened, in a second before his color shift Hazy readied her blade and armor while he prepared for yet another explosion. Nothing but the light enveloped the next star in the following moment. Hazy's layers of armor slowly burned away, and once all was burned, there was no one in front of her. She slightly panicked, looking out into the horizon for any new trace of him, nothing. She let go of her blade, she refused to believe he would manage to find his way back.

She sent a message to all the Inka, they must find Ferd. A torn off bead from a series of strings, this was urgent for them all. No more theatrics, no more careless observation. A testament cannot be broken at any cost, even after the death of the Prismatic God.

At the edge of a massive walker, Ally was sat with feet dangling in the air in the back, clinging to a wilting flower close to her chest.
