Tenderly, slowly and carefully, Ferd sat down next to her, his feet dangling down from the walker's titanic height. He was nervous, shook up, he couldn't answer why.

"So... How have you been?" Ally turned to him with a smile.

"Okay." He answered quickly, looking out into the horizon.

"You seem nervous." She commented on his twitching fingers, "Or were you just abusing substances again, who knows, I don't really care about that."

They both looked at the two suns.

"Say..." Ally took a deep breath.

"You changed." She looked at him with a creepy smile, "You changed, Ferd."

Ferd's eyes locked onto hers.

"Everything changed three years ago, but you kept at it. You grew, you changed!" She held his hand firmly, "Ferd! Life really sucked before you came along! I figured it out, my life was meant to revolve around yours!"

He didn't say a word, but he got a weird ick from what was going on. Despite his uneasy feeling, he didn't let go from her grip.

"It's no coincidence I found you back in that frozen wasteland, is it?" She continued, "You might not even remember, but we were close friends when we were kids. In that little boring hometown, when I was nobody but just a Sam."

Dread fell over on Ferd.

"We were meant to be together, Ferd!" She let go of his hand, ecstatic, "We're over with the act of adventures, we are over with the act of childhood, we are over it all. What remains is, to fulfill something important."

"I was hesitant, I truly was. Repressing those feelings over the years, but I realized there was no point in doing that. This is our life now, and truthfully, what am I without you, Ferd? Just a leader of this boring town? Just a caring figure of the daughter? No, I refuse everything, I only want to be yours."

"We could do anything together, escape from this stupid town and wander into the universe, both of us. Nothing would ever get boring that way. Using my heart I can do anything you want me to, anything. We're free, Ferd. We can do anything we want! No more of the prophecy to follow, and no more of the stupid queen to follow. It's over, do you realize it too?"

He stared at her blankly, unable to come to a conclusion. It's like as if his mind was split in half, each running with their own stream of thoughts. Darkness shrouded him emotionally, the suns just below the horizon.

"Answer!" She got really close to him, "I want to hear you speak! At least once! Don't be such an idiot, you gotta realize this too, right?! The love, don't you share the feeling, too?!"

He slightly pushed her away, mumbling under his breath, "Don't..."

She was slightly provoked by that, "Hey... What do you think you are doing?!"

"Too... Close." He caught some words.

She got close to his face, again. "Are you still not over that old hag or what?"

In a swift move, Ferd tackled her onto the floor, and on top of her his hands wrapped her neck. She yelped, holding Ferd's wrists trying to get him to let up as he choked her heartlessly. His grip only got tighter and tighter, his nails digging into her skin. She was quickly running out of breath, unable to even mutter a word. She stopped trying to get a grasp on his hands, and just let it happen as he poured his fear and rage onto her. Once she was on the brink of passing out, he stopped with deep breaths. He looked at his bloodied fingers, then at her in a moment of disbelief. He stood, and left without a word.

And he walked down the hallway.

And he walked...

Sammy found him with a happy smile, but he pushed her away and continued down his path.

In the bathroom, he stared at himself through an uncleaned mirror. He stared at his heavy, unwashed face, an exhausted mess, a guilty expression. He turned the tap to wash his face, but he just let the water run, the water serving as white noise in the locked bathroom. He did nothing but continue to stare at himself as all of his actions began to weight on his mind. He turned to his right hand, scarred from the gauntlet, and he punched the mirror with it with full force. The glass shattered, his right hand bled but he did not care, he lashed out at his reflection again and again. The water in the sink turned red, adored with pieces of glass. Without washing his face, he turned off the tap and left the bathroom.

He continued to walk, exhausted, leaving a trail of blood from his right hand. He marched to his room, where he sat on the worn down makeshift bed. He looked through his drawer that was next to his bed, filled with substances and a loaded gun. His body trembled from the pain his mind could not register, instead of reaching out to the drugs he reached for the gun. All his actions and decisions throughout the years raced through his mind, how he ended up in this situation. He examined the gun, his shaking right hand staining it's sleek metallic surface. 

He felt cold, heartless, like as if nothing ever mattered anymore. He felt the guilt from everything he had done. The unfortunate Ally and Houston having to deal with his sick self over the years. 

It was not worth it to stay alive. Not after everything he did. Everything caught up to him like the wind.

He quickly placed the barrel of the pistol onto his chin. Hesitant, nervous, sweating, he didn't pull the trigger just yet, but when was about to... It's as if his hand snapped away from the pistol. He would only aim at himself again, but his self refused. The injuries were slowly catching up to him, his body was gonna let go of his control soon. But he doubled down, grabbing a dagger to stab his arm with, forcing his self to overstimulate. Darkness completely shroud him, his mind was full of fog. With the pistol, the next thing he saw was only light.

*Knock knock.*

*Knock knock knock.*

"Hey, Ferd."

"Your door's locked, you aren't answering, I can't blame you. I'm sorry for what I said."

"I pried too hard, I shouldn't have acted so weirdly."

"I have no hard feelings. It's messy for all of us. Sammy told me about you after I got back up. You know... It's still your birthday, I got carried away. We cleaned the blood too, you won't need to stress over that when you choose to come out."

"Sammy managed to bake you a whole cake, she's got a talent for that. I think you already knew that, but she has really outdone herself with this one. I'll leave a slice for you by the door, take as much time as you need."

"Don't do anything stupid, please. You're strong, that's what everyone thinks. You pulled through the impossible multiple times, and like, I still can't help but feel concerned. It's rough."

"If you come out of your room, we won't need to talk. We can just go on with our stuff, act like as if nothing ever happened this day."

"I just want you to know... Everything's okay, Ferd. Please be safe. Have a good night."

Ally's silhouette from beneath the door walked away as she gently placed the plate by his door. Unbeknownst to everyone, Ferd's room was stained with blood, and his body laid lifeless on the floor with a mutilated head.
