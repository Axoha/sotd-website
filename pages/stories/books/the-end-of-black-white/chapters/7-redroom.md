It was dark, so dark. Nothing to see, just a faintly lit floor of checkered black and white marble. From just a few glowing strings came the soul of Ferd, formed around what looked like nerves of a human. Immediately after getting control of himself back, he stood and swiftly roamed around the place, wondering where he even is. The room itself was small, the darkness made it seem way larger. He was confused, barely panicked, he touched the reflective walls, only to stop and take a moment to look at himself. A painful disfigured face, scarred, like as if he already forgot what he had done.

He looked behind him. There stood a tall black figure with four eyes and long white hair. He nervously stared at it, but it had no words to say.

"What are you." He looked down while saying that, unable to keep eye contact.

Within a blink, the figure vanished and appeared, in the same instant landing a devastating blow to his face, then his stomach, neck, consecutive punches were thrown blazingly fast, until it stopped with a final strong kick which sent him to the other side of the room. Ferd coughed up black blood, though his body didn't appear to be injured at all, the pain was very much real. Judgment was here.

"What are you!" He yelled out into the darkness, where nothing but the four eyes were visible.

It only squinted, and in a next move it summoned a lasso made of chain. It struck the ground with its tip, forming a large crack on the clean checkered floor. It approached him, every step echoed, it swung the lasso and threw it to wrap Ferd's neck in steel, and in a ruthless pull he was forced closer to it. Then came the relentless whip strikes to his back, each hurt more than the last, leaving behind large wounds that instantly healed. He crawled away, in fear, in desperation, but it only came closer and closer. Once he hit the wall, he covered his face, shaking his head, looking down before the next strike came...

He flickered red, grabbing the chain right before it hit him with his bare hand. Through his gritted teeth he mumbled in the struggle, "Hazy...!"

In shock, it retreated instantly, letting the chain disappear.

From Ferd's body large red wings grew, he struggled to get up and escape, but then he flickered back to black and he was nothing but a vessel once more. He threw up blood again, this time a crimson red.

"You are not supposed to exist." Finally, it spoke. Hazy, in a swift move formed two black gauntlets in her arms to approach and beat him up again. Each step she took made her form polished armor around her body too, piece by piece.

Something within Ferd forced him to move, evade, and stand up. Suddenly, a golden gauntlet appeared in his right hand. It felt nothing like what he experienced years ago, though, he remembered it all.

From Hazy's fingertips came chakrams, two of them. She threw them to his shoulders , which his confused self managed to evade through instinct. The chakrams stuck to the wall with a devastating strike, forming even larger growing cracks around them. From her back she pulled out a glowing white blade, about the size of her whole body, and immediately swung it in an unavoidable instant move. Ferd was floored, again. There was a clear nervous expression on Hazy's face now. He managed to roll, get behind her, quickly get up and punch her in the back with the gauntlet. The punch left a burned mark, she suppressed her yelp in pain, and swung the sword once more to get him back down. This time she did it twice, in order to cut his gauntlet off from his body. She immediately summoned a spear in the brief time he was recovering to stab him in the chest and get rid of whatever may be interfering with Ferd. Before the tip hit his chest, he flickered red again and covered himself with wings in that instant to break the spear as it hit. Hazy immediately reacted by retreating and making clones of her, there was a clear expression of fear in her eyes this time.

With a large, heart-shaped mark, he stood up red with his large wings surrounding his body. Hazy's clones immediately lunged at him, but one by one, he vanished and appeared around each to erase them with his two golden gauntlets surrounding his arms. From his fingertips he created expendable weapons to throw, he created smoke and disorienting clouds to gain advantage. Hazy kept creating clones of herself, each with their own weapons made of black, but he was unstoppable. Overcoming her fear, she swung with her sword again to force him back on the floor, but in his protective bubble made with his other gauntlet, he grabbed the blade and shattered it. Hazy instantly grew arms from her back, summoning multiple copies of the blade, and she kept trying. No matter how hard she tried, with each swing, despite how overwhelmed he seemed to be, he found a way. This was not Ferd, this was not the Vessel. This was an Angel. He cleared the remaining clones with his own slice from a holy weapon, and after, with glowing strings from his fingertips, he snapped at her. Her extra limbs were sliced off with swift cuts from the strings, and anytime she tried to swing her blade he only shattered it again with the gauntlet. It was only a matter of time, until...

He flickered to black, Hazy in her internal panic immediately seized the moment to pin him against the wall. With her sharp claws she tore his chest wide open with the full intent of demolishing his core, but all she saw inside his body was an organic heart. The confused Ferd groaned in pain as she silently panicked. He formed another black chain to wrap his neck around with, and placed multiple black daggers in his open bleeding chest to keep the wound from recovering. Murder was not a goal, but in her case, she formed a spear to stab his heart with and erase his soul for good. As she was about to lunge at him, black and white flowers bloomed from his heart and insides. She immediately stopped.

"Not red, good, good, good." She mumbled to herself as she looked closely, dropping her spear in the process.

But then, he flickered red again, and he grabbed her by the neck and tossed her aside. The daggers and chains inside him turned to butterflies upon touch, he was free as his chest healed. Hazy's neck was burning, she looked at him with scornful eyes as she recovered.

Ferd, tainted in red, only looked at her for a moment before summoning a gauntlet in his right arm. Using the hand, he rushed to the cracks where the chakrams were, and forced the wounds of the wall open. Just the void waited beyond.

"I need to come back." Ferd mumbled to himself, "I'm coming back." He spoke in a determined manner.

As he was about to step out with his two large red wings extruded, he felt something grab his foot. Hazy's arm burned, shaking, as she refused to let go.

He turned back to her. "I don't want to kill you."

"You... can't come back... Idiot." She struggled to speak, her voice was hoarse, "You... are... no one... dead can... Impossible..."

"I'll try." He arrogantly pulled his foot out of her grip.

"You can't...!" She slammed her fist into the floor. "That's... not how... the world works...!"

He looked at her with a rather somber look. Without another word to say, he leapt into the void.
