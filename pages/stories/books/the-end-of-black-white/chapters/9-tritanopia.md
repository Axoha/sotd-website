**ARC 42 PART I - TRITANOPIA**

Once the ashes of disaster have cleared, a shooting star flew past the sky, colliding with the earth with a numbing yet quiet impact. Our hero, scarred, wondered after a long time stargazing. Without another second to waste, she jumped to her motorcycle to examine the fallen star.

She followed the weaving broken road. Turning gently, staying right at the center amidst the painted yellow crossed line. Her light flickered in the pitch black darkness of the wasteland, but of course that was no problem for her. The bike crackled, shook, on the verge of death, but as long as she rode, it would last. The star emitted a bright glow in the distance, its location was behind the mild mountains on the horizon. She checked her fuel gauge with worry, that was one thing her mind had forgotten about, but as always she could rely on her daughter. She smiled, it was almost full. The glow waited, and she patiently drove past the horizon.

The moment she got close to the fallen star, the glow vanished without a flicker or blink. She took out a pair of binoculars to see through the dark. There were heat signals, obviously, but... not exactly what she expected. With caution she walked towards the object, her left hand on her back ready to whip out a weapon if needed. Her instincts stood sharp, with glaring eyes, she walked slower and slower until she was right above the crater.

Something at the center of the crater was covered in thorns and roots. Strangely, there were muffled groans too. She jumped and slid down the dirt to whatever was obscured by plants, and with a sharp cut with a knife and a grab with the other hand, she saw...

"..." Her eyes widened.

"..." His eyes also widened.

They stared at each other for a moment, lost in thought, until she shook her head and slammed him on the floor, whipping out a shotgun from her back, fully loaded and pointed at his face.

"What are you." She asks with fury, no hesitation whatsoever.

"Hi." A strange silhouette of a small being stood right next to her, waving and smiling happily. Boasting long ruffled white hair, an impish tail, some scrappy leftover clothes, and a strange black tendril connected to his body. She waved happily with a single hand, "Who are you?"

She was confused, taking a step back. "Don't hurt me."

"We come in peace!" She bowed, "I'm Sammy! Sammy Sammy Sammy Sammy! Remember that now? Saaaaammmmmyyyyy!" If it were not evident enough, whatever that was, it was a child.

"...He is...?" Her breath hitched.

"He? That's daddy!" She gestured happily, "His name is Felix! Feeeeeeeelix! *Felix!*"

He tumbled in the vines.

"Stop." She interrupted her, then she thought for a moment.

"You should help daddy he had a bad day I think." She stood and pointed at his body.

After hesitating, she found herself back on the bike, with Felix on her back, and Sammy on Felix's back gazing at the local environment. There was no moment of silence with her around.

"Who are you? Who are you? I asked that many times! Where answer?" She pounced on his back.

"Ena." Her eyes were on the road, "Ena Lone."

"Eeeennaaaa...?" Sammy leaned in with curiosity, "That's a funny name!"

"Where do you come from?" Ena asked after breaking through her confused expressions.

"Uuuuuu..." Sammy looked at the sky with two big eyes, "Up there!" She pointed with a small hand.

"Of course, you fell from the sky." She shook her head, "Origin, what are you, where were you born."

"Uuuuuu..." She scratched her head, "A sandy thing! Adopted Mommy's walker! Errrrrrrr..." She scratched her head even more, "Place like this but not so sad!"

"Okay..." She slightly nodded, her eyes were still on the road, "Now, what are you?"

"I am dark!" Sammy smiled, wobbling her arms around.

Ena figured she wouldn't get much of an answer, "Are we neighbors? Same planet?"

"Eeeeeeee..." Sammy's chin was resting on her hand, "Maybe?"

"You share the same name with my daughter." She softly smiled, "Who's the... 'adopted mommy' you mentioned?"

"Allay!" She waved her hands as the wind hit her, "Axelotel, she very nice! When she feels bad I turn into a ball and let her bounche me around!"

"Why is she... um, *'adopted'*?" Ena looked at Sammy.

"Felix and Allay are not kissing they both take care of me." She leaned closer to Ena's face, "They sleep in the same bed but that is maybe because we are poor and in mommy's words broke ass bitches in the wasteland!"

"Hey, we'll get you back home." She smiled, "With support, obviously. I'll talk it out with Felix."

The sun shined upon the land with its first golden rays.

"Miss Ena it was epic to talk! To finally have a voice!" She loudly exclaimed after looking at the sunrise, "I cannot exist in the light, I will see you in the night!"

"Uh?" She turned around again.

"Bye bye bye!-" And so, Sammy turned into floating black petals, leaving a trail on the road as Ena drove the motorcycle.

Curious, confused, she stopped the bike. Collected each scattered petal on the asphalt and put it in her bag, then got back into driving.

"*Felix...*" She muttered his name as her eyes were on the road, "Olm, give me a break."

She finally got back to the scrapyard, her home. Already bustling with activity with fellow Resistance members. Every day was full of work, life on the frontlines as usual was nothing relaxing, but each afternoon felt blissfully rewarding, every day the sun rises. There is always a tomorrow. Ena parked her bike in the garage attached to her scrappy home, gently laid down Felix and the bag full of Sammy's petals next to him before going into work.

It was at that moment, when his breath hitched, and he woke up. His chest still full of black and white flowers, but his body, his fur was black with minor white marks.

He opened his eyes. Ena, like a goddess stood with a confused look. Just a stare evaporated any thoughts Felix had, he drooled at her round proportions. She was scarred, adored with Citadelarian trinkets as a symbol of victory one could assume. She was large, way larger than he could remember, her right hand was fixed with mechanical implementations.

"E... I...!" He couldn't say her name. "Hey...! En..."

"First time falling from the sky I'm guessing." She scoffed the weirdness away, going back to her business, "I'll be with ya in a sec..."

He stood, walked up to her. He tripped on wrenches on the floor and accidentally tackled her. They both fell over onto the ground, he was on top of her, and his hand was on her left breast. His nose bled, he was incredibly flustered and he was not letting up. Ena breathed heavily, they both looked at each other without words. He missed her, but Ena kicked him away in response, maybe too hard. With a sharp steel boot, he was already knocked out again with multiple broken bones, not to forget the internal bleeding.
