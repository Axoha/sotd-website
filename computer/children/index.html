Millions of years ago, back when the first stars just came to be... In the field of white, the palace of black, our King and the Queen loved each other to no bound.

But you knew that already, didn't you?

Before the Princess was born, they had six children before. Six of us, their greatest audience.

I was the first, the Soul, Hazy.
Then there was the Tale, G...
After that was the World, L...
And then, there was the Message, O...
Second to last was the Voice, P...
And after all that chaos settled down, the Structure arrived, D...

Finally, a more organic child, initially born without a title, Ena.
She was given the title of Love.

...

She lived the shortest, shattered across space.
The Voice, the Message, and the Tale withered away during the banishment.
And... the World and the Structure, one by one wilted away.

...

What? I bet you never knew that.
What kept me staying up for so long?

I slept maybe for a million years one time. Okay, multiple times.
Okay, I somehow never wilted away like the others.
Why am I still going? Even when it is utterly hopeless?

Let's just say...
As the one responsible for the creation of souls...

...

Seeing them live, is joy.
